Our Story

Born From a Love of
Honest Bowls.

Toku's began as a simple obsession — finding the perfect bowl of ramen in central India. When we couldn't, we built it ourselves. Our kitchen in Gwalior simmers broths for 12 hours, sources the finest Korean chili pastes, and hand-crafts noodles every morning.

We believe great ramen isn't just food. It's warmth, ritual, and a little chaos. Come hungry. Leave complete.

🔥
