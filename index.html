📋 Park Guidelines

Safety Rules

👙
Proper Swimwear RequiredShorts/swimsuits mandatory. Cotton clothes not allowed on slides.
📏
Height RestrictionsSome rides require a minimum height of 4ft. Check signage at each ride.
🍔
No Outside FoodOutside food & beverages are not permitted inside the park.
📸
No Cameras on SlidesPersonal phones/cameras not allowed on water rides for safety.
🚭
No Smoking / AlcoholStrictly prohibited throughout the park premises.
🤽
Non-SwimmersMust use a life jacket (provided free at the gate).
👶
Children Under 8Must be accompanied by an adult at all times in the water park.
💊
Health ConditionsGuests with heart conditions or pregnancy advised to avoid thrill rides.
